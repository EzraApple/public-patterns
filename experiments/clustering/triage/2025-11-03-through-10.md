Seven of the top ten candidates were labeled `investigate`; one was `routine`
and two were `unclear`. This is investigation yield, not precision against
external truth.

The queue is useful enough for a deeper agent-review experiment. Its immediate
limit is the summary boundary: flat kind counts do not show which source rows
belong to the same CAD, Fire/EMS call, or incident. The `unclear` cases also
show residual DBSCAN chaining across nearby but potentially unrelated events.
Routine multi-source enforcement batches remain a semantic-ranking problem,
not a reason to retune clustering from this window.
